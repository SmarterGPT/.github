# SmarterGPT governance

SmarterGPT was founded by Joseph Gustavson (Guffawaffle), who is its current
maintainer and GitHub organization owner. This organization provides a shared home
for the framework and its contributors. Each repository retains its history,
licenses, contributor attribution, and release contracts.

## Decisions and contributions

- Use the owning repository's issues and pull requests to propose changes.
- Record changes to architecture, trust boundaries, or cross-project contracts in
  an RFC or ADR with the relevant maintainer's explicit decision.
- Follow repository-specific review and verification requirements. A passing
  status or administrative capability alone is not permission to skip a gate.
- Grant collaborators only the repository permissions their role requires.
  Organization membership does not grant runtime policy or execution authority.
- Preserve human signing and protected publication checkpoints. Moving a project
  into this organization does not change an accepted release contract.

## Attribution and licensing

Project licenses and notices are the source of truth for use and redistribution.
Preserve Joseph Gustavson's founder attribution and all applicable contributor and
third-party notices. This governance document adds no restrictions to a project's
license and does not require contributors to assign their copyright.

ContextForge remains unpublished development. LexThority remains historical
research with no active product commitment. Neither status implies a grant of
authority by another component.
